MecanumBot

active

A small omnidirectional robot (mecanum wheels, Jetson Orin Nano, LiDAR and camera) used to study dog-inspired human–robot interaction. It detects people, can lead a person somewhere using gestures, lights or movement alone, reacts to pointing, plays fetch and searches for objects. The research question: does behaviour borrowed from dogs make a robot easier for people to understand? The software is ROS 2, with a 2D simulator and a MuJoCo digital twin for work away from the robot.

First real-world runs of the object-search behaviour

medium

The robot's explore-then-search pipeline passes its tests but has not yet run on the physical robot. Take it into a real room: tune navigation, check the camera mounting and height, find out what breaks, and turn the fixes into a repeatable launch procedure.

Biscee — emotions expressed with crests

active

Biscee is a social robot with an expressive head and three pairs of movable crests, a little like a bird's feathers. Can people read emotions such as happiness, fear or anger from crest movements alone? If so, simple non-humanlike body parts are enough to carry emotional signals. Movement patterns for several emotions and intensities exist; the next step is turning them into a validated video study.

Make the crest emotions distinguishable

small

Some emotions currently end in very similar crest postures. Redesign the movement patterns so each emotion has its own recognisable shape and timing, drawing on how birds and mammals use crests and ears to signal.

Python ROS motion design

Citizen Science Portal

working prototype

A web portal where researchers publish short questionnaire studies and members of the public take part — a way to reach far more participants than lab studies can. Researchers can draft, publish and export studies; the interface is available in English, Hungarian and German. It currently runs on a single machine.

Make it ready for the public internet

medium

Harden the deployment: secrets management, HTTPS behind a reverse proxy, database authentication, and a proper consent and data-protection notice for participants.
