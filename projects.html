Student projects

Topics I supervise for student project courses, all connected to my PhD in ethorobotics — building robots whose behaviour draws on what we know about animals, especially dogs. Each topic is a task specification: what the work is, why it matters, and what the finished system has to do. The scope can be adjusted to the course and the team; if a topic looks interesting, write to me before applying.

AI lab

Research-oriented topics on MecanumBot, a small omnidirectional robot (mecanum wheels, Jetson Orin Nano, 2D LiDAR and camera) used to study dog-inspired human–robot interaction. Its software is ROS 2, with a top-down 2D simulator and a MuJoCo digital twin for work away from the robot.
