State of the wiki

26 sources ingested. The framing, the dog model and the emotion-attribution evidence are now well supported. The perception-to-emotion link has no literature at all — see citation-backlog — and that is where the thesis' contribution sits. (Superseded — see the 2026-07-28 entries below.)

2026-07-28: sutton-barto-2018-rl fully ingested and the behaviour layer is now committed to reinforcement learning — see rl-for-behaviour-learning. The reward signal is undefined (Q14) and is the sharpest open problem in the wiki after the perception gap.

2026-07-28, later: the robot platform repositories were re-clipped and the platform turns out to be materially more capable than the wiki recorded — Jetson Orin Nano, a camera with a 17-keypoint pose pipeline, full internal-state telemetry, and a neck plus grabbers. Four claims corrected; Q2, Q4, Q7 and Q13 all move. The perception gap is now literature-only: the robot can see, but nothing in the corpus says how to read affect off a body pose.

2026-07-28, third pass: the thesis reference list resolved all ten numeric references (citation-backlog) and five primaries are now held — Panksepp ×2, Plutchik, Topál, Albuquerque. Q1 has a recommendation awaiting sign-off on emotion-model-comparison. Two corrections: [7] is a 2012 paper, not 2011; and the thesis list numbers [9] twice.

2026-07-28, fourth pass: colledanchise-ogren-behavior-trees ingested on the steer that BTs are the executive layer for RL decisions. Q9 settled in favour of behaviour trees; Q3 gains an analytic tool (MTTS/MTTF). The BT+emotion reference (Belle et al. 2019) is now the most important unheld source.

2026-07-28, fifth pass: belle-2019-programming-with-affect held — the last numeric reference. It pre-empts BT nodes that make affect first-class, but has no robot, no sensing, no learning and no evaluation. Crucially it confirms the perception gap is field-wide, not specific to this lineage.

2026-07-28, sixth pass: ortony-2022-cognitive-structure ingested — the OCC primary, in its 2022 second edition. Four new pages. It exposes a stage the wiki had never named — appraisal, the event→emotion path that every implementation in the corpus hand-labelled (new Q17, arguably sharper than Q1). It supplies an independent criterion for emotion-attribution: responsiveness and reciprocity, with domestic robots and dogs as OCC's own paradigm cases. It reframes the perception gap — surface cues may be insufficient in principle (Q19). And it contradicts the thesis twice: OCC's authors deny robots can have emotions at all, and prefer hand-coding to learning. See machine-emotion-question.

2026-07-30, ostension: topal-2008-perseverative and okumura-2020-ostension-attention ingested on Csenge's steer — the pipeline perceive ostensive cues → infer pedagogical intent → reinterpret pragmatically rather than as sampled behaviour. New page pedagogical-stance holds it. Three things moved. Ostension is not a perception topic — it gates the learning rule, which puts it on the critical path of rl-for-behaviour-learning and restructures step 7 of thesis. The pipeline is an infant competence that dogs are explicitly denied (topal-2014-ostensive) — Q20, and it needs a decision, not a source. And Q7's audio question briefly became blocking — the only cue experimentally shown to produce learning is directed speech — then Csenge confirmed the same day that the robot captures audio. Nothing in the pipeline is platform-blocked; the ostension detector (Q21) is now the most self-contained piece of work in the wiki, and nobody in either lineage has built one. Playback and microphone specification remain unknown.
