The Strange Situation Test (SST) is the paradigm running through most of this corpus — several source PDFs are even filed with sst in the filename.

Originally developed to study attachment in human infants toward their mother, adapted for dogs. It revealed that the dog–owner relationship is analogous to child–parent attachment behaviour, leading to the claim that the dog possesses a behaviour-organising 'software' that fits specifically into the human social world kovacs-fri-automaton-series.

Mechanics

Separation from the attachment figure in an unfamiliar environment evokes moderate stress and anxiety, expressed as proximity seeking (e.g. standing by the door). Reunion with the caregiver evokes contact-seeking behaviours (approach, physical contact) kovacs-fri-automaton-series.

Attachment is bi-directional: dogs show behavioural signs toward their owner, and owners perceive the relationship as an emotional bond.

Why it recurs

The SST is doing three jobs across the corpus:

  1. Evidence that dog–human social organisation is deep enough to model (miklosi-2017-ethorobotics lists attachment first among dog-derived skills for robots).
  2. A rule source. "Considering dogs' major behaviour patterns shown in the SST, we could define a limited number of rules that could control the behaviour of the simulated companion" — the SST is where the fuzzy automaton's rule base comes from (kovacs-fri-automaton-series).
  3. A test scenario. Owner/stranger presence and absence in an unfamiliar room is the worked example throughout, and the base for the exploratory behaviour model in ichikawa-numakunai-2012-roman.

Concrete instantiation

The fuzzy-automaton states derived from it — MissTheOwner, AnxietyLevel, ExplorationLevel — are driven by rules over owner presence, owner-to-dog distance, stranger-to-dog distance and place familiarity (kovacs-fri-automaton-series).

These inputs are all obtainable on robot-platform

Presence, distance and movement history are exactly what a lidar plus the DR-SPAAM leg detector provide. Unlike facial-expression recognition, the SST input set is directly implementable on the actual hardware — see the sensing warning on robot-platform and Q13 in open-questions.

Gaps

  • No primary SST source in the corpus. Topál et al. (2005), Gácsi et al. (2001) are cited throughout but not held — see citation-backlog. #needs-source
  • Attachment is described qualitatively; no operational thresholds (what distance counts as proximity seeking, over what duration).
