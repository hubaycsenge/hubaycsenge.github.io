A two-page demo paper. Thin on evidence, but it makes the single most useful claim in the corpus for this thesis' architecture: the same dog-derived behavioural design was implemented across four robots with radically different embodiments, and worked in each.

The scenario

Modelled on hearing dogs assisting deaf owners by leading them to the source of an important sound. The robots reproduce the visual communicative behaviours dogs display during such leading interactions, and successfully communicate intent and lead human partners to predetermined places.

The behavioural design has four components, standardised in a flow chart:

  1. interaction initiation with the user
  2. leading behaviour
  3. feedback
  4. goal-oriented attention-directing behaviour

The four embodiments

Robot Origin Relevant features
MogiRobi BME Mechatronics + ELTE Ethology holonomic base, 2 m/s any direction; 3-DOF head, 2-DOF neck, 1-DOF ear-tufts, 2-DOF rear antenna
Ethon MTA-ELTE Comparative Ethology holonomic base, 40 kg, 2 m/s; Kinect as "head" on 2 DOF; 1-DOF arm
Sunflower University of Hertfordshire Pioneer P3-DX base; neutral non-animated face on 4-DOF neck; LED light panel, speaker, touchscreen, retractable tray
FLASH Wrocław University of Technology balancing robot; 11-DOF EMYS expressive head; two 7-DOF WANDA arms with 4 fingers each

Why this is the load-bearing claim

The embodiments span holonomic wheeled robots with no face at all (MogiRobi, Ethon), a robot whose only expressive surfaces are an LED panel and motion (Sunflower), and a highly expressive humanoid-ish head with hands (FLASH). If the dog-derived behaviour design transfers across that range, it should transfer to robot-platform — a mecanum-drive robot whose expressive channels are omnidirectional motion and four RGB LEDs, closest to Sunflower.

This is the best available answer to Q4 in open-questions ("does the dog model survive contact with implementation?") and it supports step 4 of thesis more directly than any other source here.

Note also the holonomic / omnidirectional base recurring across MogiRobi and Ethon. Mecanum drive gives the same capability. Movement quality — approach, retreat, sidle, spin — is a real expressive channel in this lineage, used in gacsi-2016-emotion-attribution (approach, back, turn away, partly spin).

Limitations

  • A demo abstract. No experiment, no participants, no measures, no results beyond the assertion that the interactions were "effective".
  • The claim of embodiment-independence is demonstrated, not tested — there is no comparison across the four robots.
  • The underlying behavioural design is credited to Koay et al. (2013), which is not in the corpus and should be obtained. See citation-backlog.
