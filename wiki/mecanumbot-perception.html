Csenge's own package (hubaycsenge/mecanumbot_sensorprocess_smart), clipped 2026-07-28 and new to the wiki. Five ROS 2 nodes that turn lidar and camera into tracked people in map space.

This is the front end that multimodal-emotion-recognition was written as though it did not exist. It substantially changes what that page can assume.

The pipeline

scan   ──► mecanumbot_lidar_detect_people ──► dr_spaam/dets ─┐
                                                             ├─► mecanumbot_locate_detections ──► people_fusion
camera ──► mecanumbot_cam_detect_people   ──► cam_people_detections ─┘

people_fusion (geometry_msgs/PoseArray, map frame) is what the behaviour trees in mecanumbot-behaviours consume. The lidar node additionally publishes subject_pose directly for the leading behaviours.

Nodes

Node What it does
mecanumbot_lidar_detect_people DR-SPAAM on 2D lidar → tracked people as poses
mecanumbot_cam_detect_people YOLO pose (Ultralytics/PyTorch, portable) → 17-keypoint detections
mecanumbot_onboard_cam_detect_people Same output via NVIDIA DeepStream, Jetson only
mecanumbot_locate_detections Fuses camera + lidar, projects into map space
mecanumbot_detect_tennis Tennis-ball presence from the camera

The two camera detectors publish the same topic — run one or the other, not both.

The 17 keypoints change the thesis

PersonKeypoints.msg carries the 17 COCO pose keypoints — nose, eyes, ears, shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips, knees, ankles — as geometry_msgs/Pose, wrapped in CamPersonDetection with angular bounds.

The perception gap is now half closed, on the platform side only

multimodal-emotion-recognition argued that with lidar only, the input set had to be proxemic and kinematic, and that this was arguably a better fit for function-orientedness than facial-expression recognition.

Skeletal keypoints sit between those two options, and they sit in the right place. They give posture, orientation, gesture, gait and inter-personal arrangement — bodily cues — without committing to face reading. That is much closer to what a dog actually reads off a human than either alternative, and it keeps the Q5 anthropomorphism objection answered.

Two cautions. First, eyes, ears and nose are among the 17 keypoints, so a coarse head-orientation and gaze-direction signal is available — which reopens Q5 by the back door if it gets used to approximate facial attention. Second, and more important: having keypoints is not having affect. No node in this package infers emotional state. The literature gap in multimodal-emotion-recognition is untouched — references [5] and [6] of research-proposal are still missing, and there is still no source in the corpus mapping body pose to affect. The platform gap closed; the literature gap did not.

Engineering details worth keeping

Recorded because they bear on the real-time claim (Q3) and on what can be trusted.

Lidar detector. DR-SPAAM weights dr_spaam_5_on_frog.pth, trained on the FROG 2D-laser people dataset. Confidence threshold 0.45. Scans are cleaned (inf/NaN → max range), median-filtered (size 3) and resampled to 240 points. Detections falling inside an inflated keepout mask (0.2 m) are rejected. Survivors are tracked with a Kalman filter plus Hungarian assignment, and a track is published only after ≥2 hits and having exceeded 0.1 m/s at some point — so stationary false positives are suppressed.

The motion gate has a cost for this thesis

A person must have moved faster than 0.1 m/s to ever be published. A standing still, seated, or motionless human is filtered out by design. Several dog-derived scenarios in dog-human-interaction-model — separation, being scolded, the stationary phases of the attachment-sst — involve exactly that. The camera path is unaffected, so fusion partly covers it, but the subject_pose output used by the leading behaviours comes from the lidar node alone. (Inference from the documented behaviour; not flagged in the repo.)

Fusion. For each camera detection: find a lidar detection whose bearing falls inside the person's angular bounds; failing that, extrapolate range from the raw scan using the 20th percentile of valid ranges inside the bounds, so background hits do not dominate; failing that, drop it. Poses landing on occupied map cells are ray-traced outward to free space (up to 4 m) and offset by 0.5 m. Runs on a 4-thread executor.

Camera detector. yolo26n-pose.pt, CUDA when available. Detections not yielding exactly 17 keypoints are skipped. Angular bounds come from min/max keypoint x and are only filled once an AMCL pose has arrived — in topic mode no frame is processed at all until /amcl_pose arrives. The DeepStream variant hard-codes confidence at 0.3 with per-keypoint threshold 0.15 (below which keypoints are emitted as NaN).

Known problems in the package

Recorded as first-party evidence of the system's state, not as criticism:

  • DeepStream config uses absolute /home/ubuntu/ paths and must be edited per machine before that node will start.
  • mecanumbot_detect_tennis declares no ROS parameters and loads yolov8n.pt, which is not among the shipped model files.
  • RViz marker publishing is disabled — publisher commented out, publish_rviz hard-coded to False.
  • Python dependencies (torch, ultralytics, opencv-python, scipy, filterpy, transforms3d, dr_spaam, pyds/gi) are not declared in package.xml, so rosdep will not install them.
  • param/ and config/ hold identical copies of the same YAML.
  • Only the lidar node's parameters are in YAML; camera, fusion and tennis nodes rely on in-code defaults.

The tennis ball

mecanumbot_detect_tennis publishes tennis_ball_info — seconds since a tennis ball was last seen (YOLO at 320 px, COCO class 32, confidence > 0.5).

Worth flagging because it is the only node in the stack that has no obvious role in leading or navigation. A ball is the canonical object of PLAY in dog–human interaction, and play is the one context in dog-human-interaction-model's impact table with a strong positive valence on happiness. If this is groundwork for a fetch or play scenario it connects to social-competence and to the PLAY circuit — but that is a guess. Ask Csenge what it is for. #needs-source

What ostension detection would need from this stack — 2026-07-30

pedagogical-stance puts an ostension detector on the critical path of the learning architecture. Assessed against what this package actually publishes:

Cue Available here?
Directed-speech prosody — the only cue experimentally shown to produce referential learning (okumura-2020-ostension-attention) Not in this package, but the robot does capture audio (Csenge, 2026-07-30 — see robot-platform). So this is a missing node, not a missing sensor: nothing in the perception stack touches mecanumbot_audio, and an ostension detector would be the first consumer
Eye contact Approximable. Nose, eyes and ears are among the 17 keypoints, giving coarse head orientation. True gaze is not available at this resolution, and the DeepStream variant emits NaN for keypoints below 0.15 confidence — facial keypoints on a distant or turned person will be exactly the ones that drop
Gaze alternation between the robot and a referent — the cue in topal-2008-perseverative's OC condition Would need head orientation tracked over time against a known referent. Nothing here does temporal analysis of keypoints; detections are per-frame
Being the addressee rather than a bystander Needs bearing from the person to the robot — derivable from people_fusion plus head orientation. Nobody in the corpus has attempted it

Two properties of this stack work against ostension detection specifically

The motion gate. A person ostending stops moving and orients — that is much of what ostending is. The lidar node publishes a track only after it has exceeded 0.1 m/s, and subject_pose comes from the lidar node alone. The camera path covers it, so this argues that any ostension detector must be camera-primary, not fusion-primary. The warning above about motionless humans applies with extra force here.

The AMCL dependency. In topic mode the camera detector processes no frame at all until /amcl_pose arrives. An ostension detector inherits that startup coupling to localisation, which is architecturally odd for a social-signal detector — recognising that you are being addressed should not require knowing where you are on a map.

And the acceptance test comes free. okumura-2020-ostension-attention Experiments 3 and 4 supply a matched stimulus pair: the detector must fire on directed speech and not on a beep of equal duration and volume, nor on that beep with matched mouth movement. Any detector that passes gaze-following benchmarks but fails this is a salience detector. Q21.
