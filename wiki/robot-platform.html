A Turtlebot3-family robot rebuilt with mecanum-wheel drive, intended to "fit into the Turtlebot3 family". Derived from the Turtlebot3 humble packages, substantially modified for four independently driven mecanum wheels.

Substantially revised 2026-07-28

The 2026-07-27 version of this page described a Raspberry Pi robot with no camera, no microphone, no telemetry and no articulated parts, and two urgent unknowns were built on top of that. All four were wrong, and the corrections run in the same direction: the platform is considerably more capable than the wiki assumed. See mecanumbot-repos for the diff.

What the embodiment permits

Omnidirectional motion is the primary expressive channel. Mecanum drive gives holonomic movement — the same capability MogiRobi and Ethon had, and the literature already uses movement quality expressively: approach, back away, turn away, sidle, partly spin, maintain distance (gacsi-2016-emotion-attribution, lakatos-2014-emotion-attribution).

Colour and light are a second channel. Four independently controlled RGB LED panels with modes. Closest precedent in the corpus is Sunflower, whose expressive surfaces were an LED panel plus motion, and which carried the same dog-derived behaviour design as three very different robots (lakatos-2014-embodiments-demo).

A neck and two grabbers are a third channel — and this is new to the wiki. AccessMotorCmd.msg commands n_pos, gl_pos, gr_pos on /cmd_accessory_pos, and mecanumbot-behaviours already uses neck and gripper gestures as the Doglike experimental condition. A neck affords orienting, head-turning and gaze-like direction of attention, which is the single most important expressive primitive in the dog literature after approach.

What the embodiment forbids

Still no face, no eyes, no ears, no tail, and no vertical DOF. The re-expression table is materially better than it was:

Dog / prior robot signal Available here?
Approach / retreat / distance-keeping ✓ (and better — holonomic)
Turn away / orient toward ✓ — now by body or neck
Head up/down dynamically (Gácsi's anger) ✓ via neck — new
Tail wagging (MogiRobi antenna) ✗ — needs a motion or light analogue
Ear position (MogiRobi appendices) ✗
Arm raising, swinging (PeopleBot) ~ partial — grabbers, but no arm reach or elevation
Body lowering / crouching ✗ (no vertical DOF)
Vocalisation following Morton's rules depends on whether audio is playback

Q4 in open-questions is correspondingly less severe. Of the Gácsi re-expressions, approach, back, turn away, orient and motionless were already available; head up and down dynamically now is too.

The two unknowns are resolved

Both blockers from 2026-07-27 are cleared

1. There is a camera. A CSI camera on the Jetson, with a full detection pipeline already written (mecanumbot-perception). Human sensing is no longer lidar-only. What the camera yields is 17-keypoint body pose, not faces — see that page for why this is a better position for the thesis than either of the alternatives multimodal-emotion-recognition was choosing between.

2. Internal-state telemetry exists, and is rich. OpenCRState.msg carries goal and measured wheel velocities, positions, currents and accelerations, per-wheel error states, DMS distance, battery voltage and 9-axis IMU; orin_battery_state adds INA219 power-rail data. This is more than emotion-engine asked for — it includes per-wheel fault state, which is a genuine "how am I doing" signal rather than just a charge level. Q2 no longer has a data blocker; only the modelling question remains.

Still open on sensing: whether mecanumbot_audio is capture, playback or both. It decides two different things — capture would open vocal affect input, playback would enable Morton's-rules vocalisation (dog-human-interaction-model), and the corpus uses vocalisation heavily. Nothing in the clips settles it. #needs-source

Consequences for the thesis

  • multimodal-emotion-recognition must be rewritten around body pose. The platform gap closed; the literature gap did not.
  • rl-for-behaviour-learning's draft MDP was built for a lidar-only robot. Both the state and the action set expand — see that page.
  • validation-study inherits three implemented conditions from mecanumbot-behaviours, and a per-user topic recording scheme service from mecanumbot_msgs for data capture.
  • Q10 (automatic behaviour coding) gets an unexpected boost: mecanumbot_msgs defines SimBehaviorEvaluation and SimDetectionEvaluation, per-scenario automatic verdicts with unsafe-motion, proximity, wrong-target and stale-detection flags. Automated scoring infrastructure already exists.

To confirm with Csenge

  • Has the full stack been run end to end on the robot, and which nodes have actually executed on hardware rather than merely built?
  • Is mecanumbot_audio capture, playback, or both? Is there a microphone? A speaker?
  • Camera specification — resolution, frame rate, FOV, mounting height and pitch. Mounting matters: a low camera sees legs and torsos, not faces.
  • Commit SHAs and licences for the three repos recording neither (mecanumbot-repos).
  • LED mode and color enumerations.
  • Measured latency: scan/frame → detection → fusion → tick → LED, neck or motion response. This number is the real-time claim (Q3), and the stack is now heavy enough — two neural detectors plus fusion — that it is no longer obviously cheap.
