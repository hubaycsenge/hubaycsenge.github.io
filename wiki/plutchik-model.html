Candidate base model #2. The corpus ingest weakened its case — not on merit, but because the surrounding literature declines to use it. Reading the primary has substantially strengthened it again, on grounds the corpus could not have supplied.

Bibliographic identification — corrected

The wiki previously guessed reference [9] was Plutchik (2001), The nature of emotions, American Scientist 89(4). That was wrong. Csenge's thesis reference list gives:

Plutchik, R. (1982). A psychoevolutionary theory of emotions. Social Science Information 21(4–5), 529–553.

Held and read → plutchik-1982-psychoevolutionary. The 2001 American Scientist paper is a separate, later popular summary, cited in that form by szabo-2011-emotional-engine and gacsi-2016-emotion-attribution — so both exist and the wiki should keep them distinct.

The model, now first-hand

Eight basic prototype adaptive patterns, derived from ethological behaviour lists rather than from introspection, in four bipolar pairs:

Pair
Incorporation ↔ Rejection Protection ↔ Destruction
Reproduction ↔ Reintegration Orientation ↔ Exploration

Three languages for the same state — and the third is the reason this model matters here:

Subjective Behavioural Functional
Fear, Terror Withdrawing; escaping Protection
Anger, Rage Attacking; biting Destruction
Joy, Ecstasy Mating; possessing Reproduction
Sadness, Grief Crying for help Reintegration
Acceptance, Trust Pair bonding; grooming Affiliation
Disgust, Loathing Vomiting; defecating Rejection
Anticipation Examining; mapping Exploration
Surprise Stopping; freezing Orientation

Three structural dimensions: intensity (the vertical axis of the "emotion solid" cone), similarity (circular order), polarity (opposites). Adjacent pairs combine into primary dyads — love, submission, awe, disappointment, remorse, contempt, aggressiveness, optimism. So the wheel is confirmed, and it is no longer [unsourced].

The causal chain: stimulus event → inferred cognition → feeling → behaviour → function.

Where it beats Panksepp for this thesis

An emotion model already written in the functional language

function-orientedness is the methodological commitment of ethorobotics — form and behaviour follow use, not human likeness. Plutchik's primitives are behaviours individuated by what they accomplish. That is the same commitment, arriving from emotion theory in 1982.

Three concrete consequences for robot-platform:

  1. The behavioural column is directly implementable. Withdrawing, stopping/freezing, examining/mapping, attacking map onto motion primitives the robot has; they need no face. Compare the re-expression table in dog-human-interaction-model, which had to be derived by hand.
  2. Intensity is native. szabo-2011-emotional-engine hand-built saturation levels, peak widths and decline rates because its base model had no intensity. Plutchik's cone has it as an axis.
  3. Surprise exists here and has no Panksepp circuit. Szabó's implemented state set is happiness, despair, fear, anger, surprise — and surprise is a Plutchik primary (→ Orientation) with no counterpart among the seven circuits. The "Panksepp-grounded" engine in the corpus is already partly Plutchik-shaped and nobody said so.

Where it loses

  • No neural grounding whatever. 1982, pre-dating affective neuroscience. Where Panksepp can point to circuits, stimulation sites and neurochemistry, Plutchik points to factor analyses of emotion words.
  • The empirical support is untraceable from the primary — see the warning on plutchik-1982-psychoevolutionary. Every study it reports is cited only to Plutchik (1980).
  • Nobody in this lineage builds on it. szabo-2011-emotional-engine cites it to distance itself; gacsi-2016-emotion-attribution cites it in passing for a four-emotion set and for the mammalian-homologue point. Choosing it means leaving the tradition with no implementation to inherit and no local expertise.
  • Continuous states need discretising before a behaviour tree can branch.
  • Descriptive, not mechanistic — it says little about computing a state from sensor input, which is this thesis' hard part.

On internal state

Plutchik separates emotion (external stimulus, presence of a survival-relevant event, induced after evaluation) from motivation (changing internal states, absence of homeostatically significant stimuli, rhythmic). Battery charge is, on his account, motivation rather than emotion.

That converges with Panksepp's split of homeostatic from emotional affects (panksepp-2005-affective-consciousness). Two independently derived models agreeing that internal state is a different category is the best-supported input Q2 has — see open-questions.

On learning

"The basic emotions are not learned, but are a direct expression of the genetic potentials of an individual." Learning affects only the expression, the mixtures and their strength — including via "the reinforcement history of an individual". Consistent with the split in rl-for-behaviour-learning: engine hand-authored, behaviour policy learnt.
