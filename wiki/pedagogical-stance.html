The interpretive mode a learner enters when it judges that a demonstration is addressed to it and intended to teach — and in which it encodes what it sees as a generalisable rule rather than as one observation of how the world happens to be right now.

Named here on 2026-07-30 to hold the target Csenge specified for the engine:

perceive ostensive cues → infer that this demonstration or reward signal is pedagogically intended → reinterpret it pragmatically rather than as sampled behaviour

The wiki had ostensive-signalling for the first stage and nothing for the second and third. This page is the second and third.

The core distinction

The same physical event supports two readings, and which one the learner takes is determined by the communicative framing, not by the event (topal-2008-perseverative):

Non-ostensive framing Ostensive framing
What is encoded episodic — "the object is now under A" generic — "this kind of object is found in A"
Scope this token, here, now the kind, in general
Exposures needed several one or few
Memory system episodic; decays semantic; persists
Serial position effect recency primacy
Status of the observation a sample from the world a claim about the world

The last row is the one that matters for rl-for-behaviour-learning. A sample gets averaged in. A claim does not.

The evidence, in three parts

1. The switch exists, and the framing alone flips it. topal-2008-perseverative held the demonstration, the motor experience and the object constant, and varied only whether the demonstrator made eye contact, used infant-directed speech and alternated gaze. Perseverative search errors went from 0.81 to 0.48 (noncommunicative) and 0.41 (nonsocial) — the same hands doing the same hiding, read as a lesson in one condition and as an event in the others.

2. It is not attention. okumura-2020-ostension-attention separated orienting from learning across seven conditions (N = 140). Shivering, a beep, and a beep with matched mouth movement all produced gaze-following as strongly as infant-directed speech did — and none of them produced any object learning. Only the ostensive cue did. Salience gets the head to turn; ostension changes what is stored.

3. Both were reported by the failure case, not the success case. Topál's infants entered the stance and were wrong to. That is the reason the effect is visible at all — see the warning below.

Why the wiki needs this as a separate concept

ostensive-signalling answers how does the robot know it is being addressed. It says nothing about what the robot should then do differently, and the answer is not "pay more attention" — that is exactly the hypothesis okumura-2020-ostension-attention rules out. The answer is switch how the observation is interpreted, and that is a claim about the learning rule, not about the perception front end.

This puts ostension on the critical path of rl-for-behaviour-learning rather than in the perception chapter, which is a structural change to thesis.

Pedagogy claim RL reading
Ostensive observations are generic the update should generalise across the kind, i.e. over a feature abstraction, not over the specific state visited
One or few exposures suffice a high effective learning rate, or a one-shot write, not incremental averaging at α
Semantic, persisting; primacy not recency it should not be overwritten by subsequent contradicting episodes at the normal rate — which is precisely why the B-trials fail

Three things this immediately makes concrete, all of which were vague before:

  • Q14 (scalar reward) acquires a second dimension. The question was what collapses the emotional state into a number. The pipeline adds: a reward is not only a magnitude, it also has an interpretive status. A treat given silently is one sample of R. A treat given with eye contact and dog-directed speech is a statement about the policy. Nothing in reward-design can express that difference, and the reward hypothesis (§3.2) has no slot for it.
  • The A(s) argument gets a companion. rl-for-behaviour-learning keeps the ethology in the admissible action set and learns the selection. The pedagogical stance says the learning rate is also a place where ethology lives — dogs and infants do not update uniformly, they update differently depending on whether they were addressed.
  • Shaping is already this, unacknowledged. reward-design's remedy for sparse reward is shaping, which in practice means a human deliberately signalling to the learner. That signalling is ostensive. The wiki has been assuming an ostension-blind learner and an ostension-using trainer.

Can a dog-modelled robot have this stance?

The competence the pipeline needs is documented in infants and explicitly denied in dogs

topal-2014-ostensive is unambiguous: human ostensive signals are "insufficient to create an 'infant-like' expectation to receive generic information" in dogs. What ostension induces in a dog is a "ready-to-learn or ready-to-obey" attitude that is "largely tied to the here and now" — dogs meet two of the three criteria for being a pedagogical recipient and fail the third, and the third is the one this pipeline is built on.

So the pipeline as specified is an infant competence. The thesis' model species does not have it.

This is not a contradiction between sources — Topál et al. are authors of both (topal-2008-perseverative is the infant result; the chapter reports the dog comparison), and they draw the contrast deliberately. It is a design tension, and it has three possible resolutions, none yet taken:

  1. Build the dog version. Ostension gates obedience and here-and-now responsiveness, not generic encoding. Best supported by the ethology, consistent with dog-human-interaction-model, and it gives up the third stage of the pipeline — the interesting one.
  2. Build the infant version and say so. The robot exceeds its model species on one axis. Defensible — function-orientedness never required fidelity to dog cognition, only that the design be justified by function — but it breaks the "dog as model" argument at exactly the point where the thesis leans on it, and invites the question of why the model species is a dog at all.
  3. Treat it as the empirical question. Whether an artificial agent that encodes generically from ostensive cues is more or less legible to untrained humans than one that does not, is measurable on robot-platform and nobody has measured it. This is the most thesis-shaped of the three.

Recorded as Q20 in open-questions. It needs Csenge's decision, and the honest position until then is that the wiki does not know.

The nearest available evidence is Topál et al. (2009a) — the dog A-not-B study, cited in topal-2014-ostensive §11.3.1.3 for dogs selecting an empty container after ostensive cueing. Almost certainly the direct dog counterpart of topal-2008-perseverative, run by overlapping authors. Not held. It is now the single highest-value acquisition for this line and is at the top of citation-backlog.

The failure mode is not a bug in the implementation

Every result on this page is the stance misfiring

topal-2008-perseverative does not show infants learning something true. It shows them learning something false, because they were addressed. The pedagogical stance is visible in the data only where it produces error.

The dog literature is the same shape: ostensively cued instructions override the animal's own evidence — deceptive pointing, empty containers, the smaller food quantity (topal-2014-ostensive).

A robot with this stage in its pipeline will therefore prefer an ostensively delivered instruction over its own perception, by construction, and the more effectively the stage works the more strongly it will do so. For a robot operating around untrained people in a real environment that is a safety property, not a curiosity. It should be a designed and bounded decision — a hand-authored override in the T_safe subtree (behaviour-trees, colledanchise-ogren-behavior-trees §8.2) rather than an emergent one.

Read the other way, as ostensive-signalling already records, this is the same mechanism that makes shaping work. Trainability and manipulability are one property, and this page is where the thesis should say so.

What has to exist for the pipeline to run

Stage by stage, against what robot-platform actually has:

Stage Needs Status
Detect ostensive cues dog-directed / infant-directed speech → microphone; eye contact → gaze, or head orientation as a proxy Available. Csenge confirmed 2026-07-30 that the robot captures audio (robot-platform), so the cue with direct experimental backing is on the hardware. Head orientation is derivable from the nose/eye/ear keypoints on mecanumbot-perception; true gaze is not
Discriminate ostension from salience must fire on IDS, must not fire on a matched beep The acceptance test is specified by okumura-2020-ostension-attention Exp 3–4. Nothing built. Q21
Identify that this agent is addressed address detection, not just presence Unattempted anywhere in the corpus
Switch the interpretive mode a gated learning rule, per the table above Unattempted; no prior art found

No stage is platform-blocked. Stage 1 was, for about two hours on 2026-07-30, and is not. The remaining constraint on it is signal quality rather than availability: microphone count, placement and sample rate are unrecorded, and recovering a fundamental-frequency contour at interaction distance over a running mecanum drive is not the same problem as close-talk audio. That is a feasibility question to measure, not a blocker to wait on.

Stage 1 is now the obvious first build, and it is self-contained

It needs no decision on Q20 — whether the robot should end up with the dog version or the infant version of the stance, the detector is the same. It has an experimentally specified pass/fail criterion. It uses two signals the platform already produces. And nothing in the corpus has built one, in either the ethorobotics or the affective-computing lineage.

A detector over prosody plus head-orientation-toward-robot, scored against the okumura-2020-ostension-attention stimulus pair, is a complete small experiment that yields the gate everything else on this page depends on.

Stages 2–4 are research, not engineering, and stage 4 is where Q20 has to be answered before anything can be written.
